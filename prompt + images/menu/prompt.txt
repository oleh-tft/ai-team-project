Create a visually stunning, high-end menu for "Tokayo" using A4 paper format, an exclusive Japanese luxury sushi restaurant. The design should exude elegance, sophistication, and a modern aesthetic.
Style and Layout:

Background: Use a sleek, matte black background to emphasize luxury and contrast with the vibrant colors of the sushi.
Typography: Utilize clean, minimalist, and high-end typography. Consider a combination of a bold, modern sans-serif font for headings and an elegant serif font for descriptions.
Color Scheme: Incorporate a refined color palette with gold or silver accents to highlight the luxury aspect. Use soft, warm lighting effects to create a welcoming yet upscale ambiance.
Positioning: Arrange the menu items in a balanced, symmetrical layout. Each dish should have its own dedicated space, with the name prominently displayed above a hyper-realistic image of the sushi.
Images:

For each sushi item include, hyper-detailed, ultra-real, modern food ad photography, perfectly styled sushi images. The images should be vibrant, detailed, and appetizing, showcasing the freshness and quality of the ingredients.
For a Nigiri include 2 pieces, For a roll include 6 pieces. They must be placed on a black round plate.
Each sushi image should be positioned next to or above its description, ensuring the visuals are the focal point of the menu.
