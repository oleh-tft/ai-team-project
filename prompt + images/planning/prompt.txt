Create a hyper-realistic, architect-grade apartment floor plan (scheme) based on the specifications below. The result should look like a professional real-estate or architectural presentation, clean, accurate, and visually premium.

Overall Concept

Design a modern, well-balanced apartment layout with a logical flow between spaces. The plan must clearly show room functions, proportions, entrances, and circulation.

The apartment must include:

1 regular bedroom

1 master bedroom

Kitchen connected to the living room (open-plan studio)

1 bathroom

1 hallway (main circulation space)

At least 1 balcony

Style & Visual Quality

Rendering Style:

Hyper-realistic architectural visualization

Clean, professional, high-end real-estate brochure look

Precise lines, soft shadows, realistic lighting, correct perspective

Color Palette:

Neutral architectural tones: soft whites, warm beige, light wood textures, muted greys

Subtle contrast between rooms for clarity

No overly saturated colors

Materials & Textures:

Light wood flooring in living areas and bedrooms

Neutral tile in bathroom and kitchen zone

Glass elements for balcony doors

Minimalistic modern interior style

Layout & Structure

Hallway (Entrance & Circulation):

The apartment entrance opens directly into a hallway, not into any room

The hallway provides direct access to all main areas: bedrooms, bathroom, and living zone

Clean, straight circulation with no awkward angles

Living Room + Kitchen (Open Plan):

Kitchen must be connected to the living room in a single open space

Kitchen zone clearly separated visually using layout or flooring change

Living room oriented toward natural light

Balcony access from either the living room or kitchen area

Master Bedroom:

Larger than the regular bedroom

Clear bed placement, wardrobes, and walking space

If possible, direct or visual proximity to the balcony

Solid bedroom door (no glass doors)

Regular Bedroom:

Smaller than the master bedroom

Accessible only from the hallway (not through another room)

Suitable for guest room, child’s room, or home office

Bathroom:

Single combined bathroom (toilet + shower/bath)

Positioned near the hallway for easy access from all rooms

Realistic sanitary layout: sink, toilet, shower or bathtub

Balcony:

At least one balcony

Must have a logical door placement from the living room or master bedroom

Exterior feel with railing, light, and outdoor flooring

Text & Labels

Each room must be clearly labeled directly on the plan with clean, modern typography:

“Living Room”

“Kitchen”

“Master Bedroom”

“Bedroom”

“Bathroom”

“Hallway”

“Balcony”

Labels must be readable but not overpower the design

Perspective & Composition

Top-down or slightly angled architectural view

Correct proportions and realistic scale of furniture

Balanced composition with clean margins

No unnecessary decorative elements

Quality Requirements

Ultra-high resolution

No distorted geometry

No overlapping rooms

No doors through other bedrooms

No entrances directly into the bathroom

Logical circulation and functional realism
